WHAT GOES BACK INTO public_html
===============================

Almost nothing. The 403 error was NOT caused by missing files.

Your public_html is EMPTY. Every address checked - index.html, robots.txt,
sitemap.xml, even the zip itself - returns "not found". A 403 at the root is
simply Apache saying "this directory has no index page and I will not list its
contents". The new site files never arrived on the server.

So: do NOT put WordPress back. Restoring it would only restore the old broken
site, along with its 21 known security vulnerabilities.


THE ONE THING WORTH RESTORING
-----------------------------

  .well-known/          (included in this folder)

This is how Let's Encrypt proves you own the domain when it renews your HTTPS
certificate. It is empty of files by design - only the folder needs to exist.

This is NOT urgent. Your current certificate is valid until 4 October 2026, and
Hostinger normally recreates this folder by itself at renewal time. Restoring it
now just removes any doubt.
