About

I am Aditya Dave, and at the heart of everything I do is a simple truth: I am endlessly curious.

I have always believed that life is about exploring, learning and understanding. It is that curiosity that led me from medicine, where I started as a doctor, to the world of strategy and investment. Each new step has been an attempt to understand the bigger picture — how business, healthcare and innovation can come together to make a difference.

I have had the opportunity to work with some truly wonderful organisations: Boston Consulting Group, B Capital and Accenture Strategy. These experiences taught me that it is not enough to just have the right answers. It is about asking the right questions, finding the connections, and having the courage to act on them.

What I write about here

My focus for this space is to voice my opinions on the future of investing, strategy, and healthcare in India. Three questions run underneath most of what I write:

  • Why does Indian healthcare resist scale? Every founder believes the bottleneck is capital. Having seen the system from inside a hospital and from inside an investment committee, I am fairly sure it is not.
  • Where does AI actually change the economics? Not the demos — the unit economics. Those are very different conversations.
  • What does good judgement look like under uncertainty? Medicine and investing turn out to be the same discipline wearing different clothes.

If you are ever curious about investing, strategy or healthcare, I would be very, very excited to have a conversation with you.
